SOP-001: Human Review Workflow

Purpose: Ensure critical clinical or surveillance classifications cannot be finalized without an explicit cryptographic human authorization.

Procedure:
1. At inference nodes labeled 'HUMAN_GATE', pipeline emits a signed hold event and halts further action.
2. A Reviewer obtains the hold event, reviews inputs and logs, then issues an authorization token by signing the hold event with their GPG key.
3. The system verifies the human signature against the repo's authorized reviewer keys and only then releases the final classification.

Enforcement:
- The pipeline enforces programmatic pauses by verifying a valid signed token before proceeding.
- All hold events and signatures are recorded in the immutable audit sink.
