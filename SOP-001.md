# SOP-001: Human-in-Loop Gate

Purpose
- Provide an auditable, cryptographically enforced human approval gate prior to finalizing clinical or surveillance inferences.

Gate criteria
- All upstream statistical validations have passed (MSE, t-test alpha=0.01, Cohen's d within expected ranges)
- All artifacts have valid SHA-256 hashes recorded in manifests
- Artifact provenance has been signed (Sigstore or GPG) and verification succeeded

Procedure
1. CI produces candidate artifact and a verification report (buildinfo.json, manifest.json, signatures)
2. Designated Operator reviews report and signs the approval token using their signing method
3. CI verifies the sign-off token; if valid, the artifact is promoted to release

Operator roles and permissions
- Principal Investigator: full gate rights
- Authorized Operator: may sign routine releases (per policy)

Emergency override
- SOP documents a temporary override flow that requires multi-party sign-off and full post-incident audit (see incident playbook in docs/incident_playbook.md)
